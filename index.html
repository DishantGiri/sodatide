SYNBIOTIC FORMULA

How Sodatide Works

This supplement uses two connected forms of microbiome support.

1
First, chicory root inulin and potato resistant starch act as prebiotic substrates. These carbohydrates resist complete digestion in the upper gastrointestinal tract and become available for fermentation by intestinal microbes.
2
Second, the probiotic blend supplies beneficial bacterial cultures. Pairing prebiotics with probiotics is commonly called a synbiotic approach because one part supplies microorganisms while the other helps create a favorable nutritional environment for them.

USAGE & ROUTINE

How to Take Sodatide

Adults should take one capsule daily with eight ounces of water, preferably in the morning before a meal. Refrigerate the bottle after opening to help preserve the probiotic formula.

For a simpler routine:

STEP 1

Keep the unopened supply in the storage conditions stated on the label.

STEP 2

Refrigerate the active bottle after opening.

STEP 3

Take one capsule at roughly the same time each day.

STEP 4

Do not exceed the labeled serving to accelerate results.

STEP 5

Pair use with regular meals, hydration, movement, and adequate sleep.

Always follow the directions printed on the bottle you receive, as online instructions and product labels can change.

SAFETY & PRECAUTIONS

Safety Information and Possible Side Effects

Sodatide side effects may include temporary gas, bloating, abdominal discomfort, or changes in bowel habits, particularly when introducing fermentable fiber or probiotics. Starting a new gut-focused product can affect people differently.

Do not use it without professional advice if you:

Are pregnant or nursing
Are under 18
Have a serious digestive or immune condition
Take prescription medication
Have known allergies to any listed ingredient
Have been instructed to follow a medically restricted diet
Read the complete label before the first dose.
